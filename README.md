## About Me

I am a **Full Stack Product Engineer** based in India, building fast, scalable and secure applications with **Next.js, Node.js and PostgreSQL**. Currently pursuing my MCA while leading a team of **10+ developers**.

I am not a developer who just clears tickets. I take raw ideas and turn them into production ready architecture: database schemas, API pipelines, responsive UI, deployment. Complete ownership of the product, not a slice of it.

My working style is **player-coach**. Deeply hands on with the codebase, writing clean code, while mentoring the team to ship faster without breaking production. I do my best work in high growth startup environments, building things real users depend on.
